Glossary

Impressions
How many times your URL appeared in Google search results.
Clicks
How many impressions turned into actual visits. Combined with impressions → CTR.
CTR (Click-Through Rate)
Clicks ÷ impressions. The "is the title compelling?" metric. Low CTR at a good position = rewrite the title.
Average position
Where your URL ranked, averaged across impressions. Lower is better. 1–3 = real traffic; 11+ = essentially zero clicks.
Engaged sessions (GA4)
Sessions that lasted 10+ seconds, had a conversion event, or had 2+ pageviews. The "did they actually read it?" metric.
CTA click rate
Clicks on the "Try Draft and Arc" link ÷ visitors. Industry baseline is 1–3%.
